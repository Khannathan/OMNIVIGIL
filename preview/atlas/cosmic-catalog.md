# OMNIVIGIL Cosmic Catalog

A content explosion for the universe of OMNIVIGIL. Every entry has a number so you can star or cut it by number (for example "star B12, B40, W7; cut P55").

**Phases:** EF = Earthfall (home world), SR = Sunreach (solar system), SW = Starward (interstellar and intergalactic), IC = Intercontinuum (interdimensional endgame). A phase marks where an entry first appears; most keep appearing afterwards.

**Rarity:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic (one per run at most, often none).

**Already in the star-system preview:** hycean (Thalasse), eyeball (Oculus), diamond (Adamant), chthonian (Khthon), computronium (Axiom), rogue (Nox), gas giant with moons (Tempest), strange matter (Strangelet), living flesh world (Corpus). They are not repeated below.

**Design rules:** every oddity creates a choice, not just a hazard. The strangest stay rare. Strangeness scales with the phases: odd worlds in Earthfall, stellar events in Sunreach, galactic physics in Starward, reality breaking in Intercontinuum.
